Flexible Criteria The following section provides evidence for the ﬂexible criteria included in the TBL Certiﬁcate rubric. Evidence relating to team collaboration, artifacts, and class participation is distributed throughout the portfolio because these elements were embedded across the workshop activities. This section identiﬁes where that evidence can be found and provides additional evidence relating to facilitation, attendance, and professional communication. Team Collaboration Evidence of team collaboration is included throughout the Documentation of Learning, Use of Technology, Peer Review, and Public Sharing and Collaborative Development sections. During the module, I participated in team readiness assurance activities, application exercises, simultaneous reporting, gallery walks, and collaborative discussion. The WhatsApp exchanges also demonstrate that collaboration continued beyond the workshop through resource sharing, peer feedback, and support during portfolio development. Artifacts The portfolio includes artifacts from each stage of the learning process, including photographs of team activities, iRAT and tRAT questions, application exercises, simultaneous reporting, gallery walks, the complete LAMS sequence, peer feedback, lesson revisions, and collaborative WhatsApp exchanges. These artifacts are accompanied by captions and commentary explaining their connection to the module learning outcomes and my development of TBL knowledge and practice. Class Participation The Documentation of Learning includes photographs and screenshots demonstrating my participation in team discussions, application exercises, readiness assurance activities, gallery walks, and whole-class reporting. The timestamped photographs included under Attendance provide additional evidence that I was actively involved in collaborative workshop activities across all three days of the module.
