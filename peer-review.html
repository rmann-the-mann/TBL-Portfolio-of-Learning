Feedback from Patricia • Well-designed clear introduction lesson to AI use in assessments. Well done! Feedback from Ryan My feedback on Patricia’s lesson started with suggesting that the resources could be placed in Moodle and the instructions provided in LAMS. I pointed out that the pre-reading materials provided useful background on the topic. I also considered whether the first activity was intended to assess readiness or support reflection and suggested reviewing its alignment with the intended purpose. For the application activity, I identified the value of the clear directions and practical examples while offering suggestions for supporting the learner experience.

Reﬂection on Peer Review Exchanging feedback provided an opportunity to view my lesson from another educator’s perspective and identify elements that were not as clear to someone encountering it for the ﬁrst time. Patricia’s feedback resulted in two direct revisions: clearer instructions for the pre-class resources and the addition of a situational question to the readiness assurance process. Her suggestion to include a written justiﬁcation also identiﬁed a worthwhile improvement, even though I was unable to implement it within the current LAMS activity. Reviewing Patricia’s lesson was also valuable because it required me to consider the alignment among preparatory resources, instructions, activities, and the intended learning. Together, the feedback given and received reinforced the value of peer review as part of an iterative design process.
